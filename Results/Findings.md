# Key Findings

## Overview

The processed network traffic dataset was analyzed using Apache Hive after distributed processing with Hadoop MapReduce.

The analysis revealed several important characteristics of the network traffic.

---

## Finding 1

The dataset contained both normal and malicious network traffic records.

---

## Finding 2

Attack distribution analysis identified the most frequently occurring attack categories.

---

## Finding 3

Protocol-wise analysis showed the distribution of traffic across different network protocols.

---

## Finding 4

Service analysis identified the network services most frequently targeted.

---

## Finding 5

Connection flag analysis provided additional insight into network behavior and attack characteristics.

---

## Outcome

The project demonstrates how Hadoop and Hive can efficiently process large network traffic datasets to support security analysis and intrusion detection.
